my new 3d printer

tuesday, december 9, 2025 — 11:52
track: aphex twin - flim (drumless) — mute

i recently got a bambu lab p1s, and i'm loving it so far!

the print quality is amazing, and the speed is crazy too. the last 3d printer i had was held together with zipties and pumped thick abs onto an unheated bed covered in painters tape. i haven't even had a failed print with this one! i got some pet-g and tpu which i needed for robotics and am going to test them out later this week. i'll update you on the results.

unrelated, but i found this drumless version of flim by aphex twin and it's so calming. thats really all i have to say for now.
